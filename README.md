# My Favorite Series

## Mobile Suit Zeta Gundam

**Mobile Suit Zeta Gundam** is one of my favorite mecha anime series because of its complex characters, mature storytelling, and emotional intensity. 

### Why I Love It
1. **Deep Political Themes**
2. **Memorable Characters**
3. **Epic Mobile Suit Battles**
4. **Tragic Events and Character Trauma**

> “You can’t change the world without getting your hands dirty.”

Here’s a short summary:

| Character | Role | Notable Trait |
|------------|------|----------------|
| Kamille Bidan | Main Protagonist | Skilled pilot with a strong sense of justice |
| Quattro Bajeena | Veteran Pilot | Calm, mysterious, and strategic |
| Emma Sheen | AEUG Officer | Compassionate and determined |

---

**Fun Fact:**  
*Zeta Gundam* explores the cost of war and the emotional toll on soldiers — showing that even heroes can be broken by the battles they fight.
